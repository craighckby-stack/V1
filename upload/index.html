Wayback Dork

Search the Internet Archive with Google-style operators, then pull the title, links, emails, forms and more out of any archived page. Everything runs in your browser.

Search the archive

DateStatusTypeURL

Operators: site: filetype: ext: inurl: mime: status: after: before: and a leading - to exclude. Separate several extensions with |. The archive stores URLs, not page titles, so intitle: and intext: are not available.

Use this for research, journalism and checking your own sites. Archived pages can still contain personal data, so handle what you find responsibly. The Internet Archive rate-limits heavy use, so keep limits modest.
